Rookahead is made by one developer in India, and everything in it is free for everyone: every review, every puzzle, every feature.

If Rookahead helps your chess, a small donation keeps it growing. There's no obligation, and the app stays the same whether you give or not.

## Questions

### Where does the money go?

The domain, the Play Store account, the voice and tools for our videos, and most of all time to keep building: better verdicts, Explain, the phone app and what comes next.

### Do supporters get anything extra?

No. Every feature stays free for everyone. That's the point of Rookahead.

### Is Rookahead a non-profit?

No, it's a small independent project, so donations aren't tax-deductible. They go straight into Rookahead.

### Other ways to help

Share Rookahead with a friend, send your games to our [Discord](https://discord.gg/ebm3NZNHZH), star the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/rookahead/rookahead) or report a bug. It all helps.
